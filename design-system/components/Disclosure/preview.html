How energy is scored1 to 10

Energy starts from BPM and the show a track was heard on, then moves up or down one step for vocals, drums and key. Tracks with only a BPM get an estimate and a “Check energy” flag.

Low is 1–4, Building 5–7, High 8–10. Sets climb one step at a time and drop 2–3 after a peak.

How the library is builtweekly, Mondays 06:00

Each Monday the run reads the week's PBS, 3RRR and triple j playlists and the ARIA Club Chart, matches every play to TIDAL, Deezer and Beatport, and adds new tracks to the crate.

How rotation is worked outrun of 24 Oct 2026

High rotation and Vault are worked out by the weekly run from the library’s own play data.

High rotation: heard on 2 or more different shows in the run window, or on the ARIA Club Chart for 4 weeks or fewer (new or rising). Everything else heard is Standard. This rule reproduces all 294 tracks of this run.

Vault: not set yet. There are 15 days of play history; the threshold is learned once there are 8 weeks, from the 90th percentile of gaps between repeat plays and 3× the median show cadence, using the larger. Until then no track moves to Vault.
