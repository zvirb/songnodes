# DR-027 Rotation calculated from play data

- Status: Accepted (answers of 24 Oct 2026)
- Date: 24 Oct 2026 (second choices page and chat)
- Decided by: Markus

## Context
Not recorded in 17-governance.md.

## Decision
High rotation and Vault are calculated each weekly run from play data, never hard-coded. Fitted to the 24 Oct 2026 library (all 294 tracks reproduced): High = heard on 2+ different shows in the window, or on the ARIA Club Chart for 4 weeks or fewer. Vault threshold is learned once 8+ weeks of history exist: the larger of the 90th percentile of gaps between repeat plays and 3× the median show cadence.
